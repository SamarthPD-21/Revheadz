How fast are your reflexes, really? 🚦

I built a side hustle to find out.

It's called Revheadz: a drag racing game that runs in your browser. Heads-up quarter mile, two lanes, one Christmas tree. Four ambers drop, the light goes green, and the race is already half decided.

Here's the twist: every car in a class is tuned to run the exact same perfect quarter mile. The Ferrari doesn't save you. The Supra doesn't save you. Nothing you can buy saves you.

It comes down to two things:
⚡ Your reaction off the line
⚡ Hitting every shift the moment the light flashes

Leave before the green? Red light. You lose. 🔴

After every race you get a time slip that shows exactly where you won or lost, down to the millisecond. It's brutally honest.

What's inside:
🏁 35 cars and bikes, from a Supra to a Bugatti Chiron to a Formula 1 V10, each with sound built from recordings of the real engine
👻 Ghost challenges: set a run, send the link, and your friend races your exact run as a ghost car. Then they send theirs back.
🎮 Online rooms: a 4-letter code and you're racing a friend
📱 Works on your phone. No install, no sign-up.

I built it for people who think they have great reaction times.

So prove it. Post your best reaction time in the comments 👇
